# Showing the platform to a school (10 minutes)

For a school's leadership team. Use the demo site (or the demo school loaded
into your own project). Everything in it is fictional.

The quickest way: on the sign-in page press **Take a guided tour** and go
through its ten stops while you talk. Or follow this script yourself.

| Time | Who | Show | Say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Administrator | Dashboard | "The whole school on one screen: attendance, homework completion, performance, updated as teachers work." |
| 1:00 | Administrator | Students requiring academic attention | "Students whose scores are low or falling, early enough to help. Academic signals only, to start a conversation." |
| 2:00 | Administrator | Teachers → Add teacher | "Staff accounts in seconds; the temporary password is shown once, to share privately." |
| 3:00 | Administrator | Reports | "Averages by subject and class, downloadable for meetings." |
| 4:00 | Teacher | Attendance | "Mark everyone present in one tap, change the few absent, save. Parents of absent students are told." |
| 5:00 | Teacher | Assignments → New | "Homework with a due date and files; students and parents see it immediately." |
| 6:00 | Teacher | Dashboard → waiting to be graded | "Grades and feedback go to the student and their parents." |
| 7:00 | Student | Assignments, Grades | "On a phone: what's due, hand it in, see grades next to the class average." |
| 8:30 | Parent | Dashboard, Messages | "Only their own children. They can message the teachers; the school decides who can start a conversation." |
| 9:30 | Any | Language: አማርኛ | "Every screen is in Amharic too." |

Questions schools usually ask:

- **Who can see a child's grades?** The child, their linked parents, their
  teachers and the school's administrators. Never other families. See `docs/security.md`.
- **Does it work on cheap phones?** It's built phone-first and tested at
  phone widths from 375 px.
- **Do we need to install anything?** No, it runs in the browser.

After the demo, write down what they liked, what's missing and what worries
them (milestone M7).
